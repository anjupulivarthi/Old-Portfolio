About Me

I am a researcher exploring the intersection of AI-mediated communication, source authenticity, and audience psychology within moving image studies. My work investigates how synthetic cues shape human perception of media truth, alongside ongoing research in Indian Eco-Cinema and sustainable production strategies.

Research Interests

  • AI-Mediated Communication & Source Perception: Psychological processing of human vs. synthetic media sources and algorithmic authenticity.
  • Moving Image Studies: Structural cues, spectator emotion, and technological shifts in visual storytelling.
  • Indian Eco-Cinema: Environmental narratives, material waste management strategies, and eco-critical frameworks in cinema.

Education & Background

  • Graduate & Undergraduate Degrees: Mass Communication & Journalism
  • Professional Experience: Journalism, media publishing, and public relations
